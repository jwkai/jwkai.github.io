I am an MSc student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia [Software Practices Lab](https://spl.cs.ubc.ca/index.html), co-supervised by [Ron Garcia](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rxg/) and [Ohad Kammar](https://denotational.co.uk/) (University of Edinburgh).

My research interests lie in probabilistic formal methods, such as the design of probabilistic programming languages and statistical model checking. I have more than a passing interest in applications to AI safety, particularly reinforcement learning. I am also interested in the foundations of probabilistic and statistical computing, particularly in the interplay of denotational semantics of programming languages with classical mathematics, such as probability theory and functional analysis.

I previously worked with [Mina Tahmasbi Arashloo](https://mina.arashloo.net/) on probabilistic formal methods for network performance analysis, and with [Alex Summers](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~alexsumm/) on separation logics for algebraic program specifications in the [Viper](https://www.pm.inf.ethz.ch/research/viper.html) verification framework. Before that, I worked as a software developer in scientific labs and healthcare.

In the more distant past, I studied mathematics and music technology, and dabbled in experimental electronic music.
